# Resonate Journal | Design System

## 1. Core Brand Palette

### Primary & Neutral
*   **Primary Brand (Deep Indigo):** `#331A47` - Used for primary branding, titles, and strong emphasis.
*   **Body Text (Graphite):** `#4A4A4A` - Used for standard readable text.
*   **Captions (Light Slate):** `#808080` - Used for secondary text and captions.

### Surfaces
*   **Warm Sand (Surface):** `#F3EBE3` (approximate) - Used for app backgrounds and card surfaces to provide a warm, calming feel.
*   **Pearl White (Canvas):** `#FFFFFF` - Used for clean backgrounds and high contrast areas.

### Accents & Data
*   **Muted Lavender:** `#C3B4D8` - Primary accent for data visualization, progress bars, and subtle highlights.
*   **Sunlit Gold:** `#D8B46C` - Secondary accent for alerts, highlights, and secondary data.
*   **Pale Peach:** `#F2E3DB` - Used for badges, tags, and soft backgrounds.

---

## 2. Typography System

**Typeface:** Clean, modern sans-serif (e.g., Inter, SF Pro, or custom "Resonate Sans").

*   **H1 (Screen Title):** 22pt, Deep Indigo, **Bold** (e.g., "Observatory")
*   **H2 (Section Header):** 16pt, Deep Indigo, **Semi-Bold** (e.g., "Your Alignment Score")
*   **Body Text:** 14pt, Graphite, Regular (e.g., "Today I noticed...")
*   **Caption:** 12pt, Light Slate, Regular (e.g., "Unknown")
*   **Weights Used:** Regular, Semi-Bold, Bold

---

## 3. UI Components & Layout

### Global Variables
*   **Grid System:** 8px grid.
*   **Spacing Units:** 16px (standard padding/margin).
*   **Border Radius:** 20px (Clarity First) for all standard card containers and large UI elements.

### Components
*   **Card Containers:** Warm Sand or Pearl White backgrounds with 20px border radius. Subtle inner padding (usually 16px or 24px).
*   **Primary Button ("Start Reflection"):** Circular/Pill-shaped with a soft gradient (Pale Peach to Muted Lavender), centered text, and an outer glowing ring/border.
*   **Text Buttons:** Simple text with Deep Indigo color and semi-bold weight (e.g., "Edit").
*   **Badges/Tags:** Pill-shaped, Pale Peach background, small bold text (e.g., "SELF-WORTH").
*   **Data Visualization:** Smooth, curved area charts using gradients (Muted Lavender to transparent). Horizontal progress bars with rounded caps.

---

## 4. Iconography
*   **Style:** Outline style, consistent stroke weight (approx 1.5px to 2px).
*   **Color:** Deep Indigo or Graphite.
*   **Examples:** Lotus (brand/mindfulness), Bell (notifications), Settings (gear), Bar Chart (stats), Edit (pencil/box), Share (network), Favorite (star), AI Insight (lightbulb).
